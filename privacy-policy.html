Our Privacy Practices

Privacy Policy

Prayer Force USA respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through PrayerForceUSA.net, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Effective August 10, 2026

Information You Provide

When you sign up to participate in Prayer Force USA, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your selected Prayer Force USA branch preference

We also receive information you voluntarily include when you contact us by email or otherwise communicate with us.

Please do not send sensitive personal, financial, medical, or emergency information through the website or ordinary email.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit PrayerForceUSA.net, certain technical and usage information may be collected automatically through website hosting, security, and analytics services. Depending on the service and your settings, this may include:

  • Your Internet Protocol address
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited and referring pages
  • Dates and times of visits
  • General geographic information derived from technical data
  • Interactions with the website

Prayer Force USA currently uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage and improve the site.

How We Use Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Process and confirm Prayer Force USA signups
  • Maintain participant and email subscriber records
  • Send communications you requested or agreed to receive
  • Direct communications according to your selected branch preference
  • Respond to questions and messages
  • Operate, secure, maintain, and improve the website
  • Understand general patterns of website use
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect legitimate rights and safety

Email Communications

Prayer Force USA uses a confirmation process for new email subscribers. You must confirm your subscription before regular emails are sent.

You may unsubscribe from email communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in those messages. We may retain limited records when reasonably necessary to honor an unsubscribe request, maintain accurate records, or satisfy legal obligations.

Service Providers

Prayer Force USA relies on third-party service providers to operate the website and communications. These currently include:

  • Mailchimp, an Intuit service, for signup processing, subscriber records, and email communications
  • Netlify for website hosting and related technical services
  • Google Analytics for website traffic and usage measurement

These providers may process information on our behalf or in connection with their services. Their handling of information is also governed by their respective terms, privacy notices, and service settings.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights or safety, prevent misuse, or support the operation of Prayer Force USA through service providers.

Cookies and Analytics Choices

PrayerForceUSA.net and its service providers may use cookies or similar technologies for website operation, security, and analytics.

Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their privacy settings. Restricting cookies may affect certain website functions. You may also learn about Google’s privacy practices and available controls through Google’s privacy resources.

Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including maintaining subscriber records, providing requested communications, responding to inquiries, protecting the website, and meeting applicable obligations.

Information maintained by third-party providers may also be subject to their retention practices and the settings available to Prayer Force USA.

Third-Party Websites

PrayerForceUSA.net may link to YouTube and other websites or services that Prayer Force USA does not control. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. A link does not make Prayer Force USA responsible for another website’s collection or use of information.

Children’s Privacy

PrayerForceUSA.net is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. Prayer Force USA does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information under our control. However, no website, email transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices and Requests

You may contact Prayer Force USA to ask about personal information you have provided, request a correction, request deletion, or change your communication preferences.

Some information may need to be retained when reasonably necessary to honor an unsubscribe request, protect the website, maintain appropriate records, or comply with applicable obligations.

Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or information practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will show when the policy was most recently updated.

If Prayer Force USA begins collecting additional information—such as voluntary alternate contact methods for emergency communications—this policy should be updated before or when that collection begins.
