Our Standards

Content & Independence

This statement explains how Prayer Force USA approaches public commentary, outside material, organizational independence, and the responsible use of my content and likeness.

We Remain Independent

Prayer Force USA is an independent initiative. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or controlled by any individual, organization, political party, candidate, campaign, or political organization unless we expressly state otherwise.

Our conclusions, commentary, and prayers are our own. They are guided by the purpose and convictions of Prayer Force USA—not by loyalty to a party, personality, organization, or outside interest.

We Address Public Life

Prayer Force USA addresses current events and matters of public concern from a prayer-focused perspective. That may include political leaders, government actions, public policy, elections, cultural developments, and other matters affecting our nation.

Discussing, quoting, criticizing, commending, or praying about a person or organization should not be understood as a blanket endorsement or rejection of that person, organization, or everything associated with them. When Prayer Force USA intends to express a specific endorsement, affiliation, or partnership, we will say so plainly.

We Use Outside Material for a Defined Purpose

Our content may include brief clips, excerpts, quotations, names, images, trademarks, or other materials involving public figures, news sources, organizations, and third parties.

We use such material for purposes that may include commentary, discussion, context, education, religious expression, and prayer. Its inclusion does not mean that the person or organization featured agrees with Prayer Force USA—or that Prayer Force USA necessarily agrees with or endorses that person, organization, or all of their views.

Third-party materials remain the property of their respective owners. We seek to identify sources and provide appropriate attribution when reasonably practical. Nothing on this page claims ownership of material belonging to others.

We Respect Legitimate Discussion of Our Work

Prayer Force USA respects legitimate quotation, commentary, criticism, reporting, and discussion of its content.

Our original work should not, however, be copied or republished in full, materially altered, deceptively edited, or presented as someone else’s work without permission, except as otherwise permitted by law.

No one may use Prayer Force USA content to falsely suggest an affiliation, sponsorship, approval, or endorsement that does not exist.

We Protect Identity and Likeness

My name, image, voice, or likeness may not be used to impersonate me, falsely attribute statements or views to me, or suggest an affiliation or endorsement that does not exist.

They also may not be used to create or distribute AI-generated, cloned, manipulated, or other synthetic representations that depict me saying or doing something I did not say or do, without my express written permission.

This protection is not intended to prevent legitimate reporting, quotation, commentary, criticism, discussion, or other lawful use. Its purpose is to guard against deception, impersonation, false attribution, and manufactured representations.

Our Aim Is Clarity

Prayer Force USA speaks plainly about matters that affect the nation and brings them before the Lord in prayer. We want people to understand whose views they are hearing, why outside material is being shown, and where Prayer Force USA stands.

Independence allows us to speak honestly. Clear attribution and responsible use help us do so faithfully.
